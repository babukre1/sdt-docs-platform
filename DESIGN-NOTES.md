# SOMNOG9 Design Notes

The UI intentionally stays restrained and uses the supplied SomNOG July 2026 Monthly Snapshot as visual direction.

Applied cues:

- predominantly white pages
- cyan / sky-blue SomNOG accent
- strong black headings
- small serif/uppercase section labels
- thin horizontal rules with a small black dot
- pale-blue information surfaces
- large white-space margins
- very limited decoration
- a subtle pale-blue curved shape on the homepage
- no glassmorphism, gradients-as-primary-decoration, heavy shadows or oversized dashboard cards

The main website remains more web-native and minimal than the PDF while preserving the document's recognizable visual language.
